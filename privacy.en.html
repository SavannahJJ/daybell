Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 September 2026 · Applies to DayBell for iOS (com.jingfanxiao.daybell)

In one sentence

What you put in your alarms stays on your phone; we never receive it. But the app includes a third-party analytics and performance-monitoring SDK (Umeng), which does send usage and device information to Umeng's servers. The two are kept separate below.

Please note: this app is not "zero data collection". If you have seen a "no tracking" claim elsewhere, this page is the accurate one — it describes what the app actually does today.

1. What stays on your device

The following is stored in the app's own storage on your device. It is not sent to us, and we have no server that could receive it:

  • Alarms — time, repeat days, the errand and note you typed, whether snooze is on.
  • People — names and cities/time zones you add by hand inside the app. The app does not read your iPhone's address book and never asks for Contacts permission.
  • Settings — interface language, work days, whether the lock screen shows details, sound choice.
  • History — one-time alarms that have finished.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. We cannot restore it, because we never had it.

2. What leaves your device

Umeng (U-App analytics, U-APM performance monitoring)

The app includes Umeng+ analytics and performance SDKs, operated by Umeng Tongxin, part of Alibaba. They are used to understand which features are used, and to diagnose crashes and freezes.

PurposeWhat is sent
Usage analytics (U-App)Launches, session length, screen views, custom events (for example a one-off integration self-check event).
Performance (U-APM)Crash and freeze stack traces, launch timing, memory and out-of-memory terminations, network request timing and failures.
Device and environmentDevice model, OS version, language and region, network type, app version, and a device identifier generated by Umeng.
Advertising-related identifiersThe Umeng SDK's own privacy manifest declares that it collects AdvertisingData. This app shows no advertising, but that declaration still applies to the SDK.

Data is sent to Umeng domains including oslogs.umeng.com, ucc.umeng.com, cnlogs.umeng.com and errnewlog.umeng.com. Umeng's own privacy policy is at umeng.com/page/policy.

Umeng's log-collection feature is disabled — the app's own logs are not uploaded.

There is currently no in-app switch to turn analytics off. If you would rather this data were not sent, the only way today is not to install the app, or to delete it. We do not think that is a good state of affairs, and an opt-out is planned.

Services provided by Apple

  • System alarms (AlarmKit) — alarms are registered with iOS, which rings them and draws the ringing screen. An alarm's title is passed to the system so it can be displayed. The errand and the person's name appear on the lock screen only if you turn on "show details on the lock screen"; they are hidden by default.
  • Subscription (StoreKit) — payment is handled by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. Whether a subscription is active is read from the receipt on your device.

3. What we do not do

  • No sign-up and no account.
  • No advertising is shown.
  • We do not sell or trade your personal information.
  • We do not upload your alarms, errands or contact names to ourselves — we have no server.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, location or health data, and never request those permissions.

4. Your rights and choices

  • Delete your data — uninstalling the app removes everything stored locally.
  • Stop analytics — currently only by uninstalling (see above).
  • Access, correct, delete — to exercise these rights over data already collected by Umeng, email us and we will help raise it with them.

5. Children

DayBell is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes to what is collected will be announced in the app.
