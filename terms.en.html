1. What the alarm can and cannot do

Read this section first. This is an alarm app, and being clear about what it cannot do matters more than anything else here.

We promise none of the following:

• Ringing while the phone is off or out of battery — this is physically impossible. No code runs on a powered-off iPhone. No app can do it, including Apple's own Clock.

• Forcing the volume — ringing volume is decided by iOS and cannot be overridden by an app.

• A 100% guarantee that it rings — alarms are fired by the iOS system alarm service (AlarmKit). System state, storage problems and OS updates can all affect the outcome.

DayBell registers alarms with the iOS system alarm service, so they can ring through silent mode, Focus, a locked screen, the app being force-quit, and after a restart. Those are capabilities iOS provides — they are not guarantees we can make.

Please do not rely on DayBell as your only safeguard where the consequences are serious — catching a flight, taking medication, medical reminders. For anything that matters, set a second, independent alarm.

2. Subscription

  • DayBell offers a free first week, then an auto-renewing subscription at ¥3.50 per week (or the equivalent in your region). The price shown in the App Store inside the app is authoritative.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account, and renews automatically within 24 hours before the period ends unless cancelled before then.
  • To cancel: Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. You keep access for the remainder of the paid period.
  • Cancelling during the free trial costs nothing. If you do not cancel before the free period ends, billing begins.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple; we cannot issue them directly.

3. What happens when a subscription lapses

Alarms you have already set keep ringing. A lapsed subscription does not silence them and does not delete them.

What lapsing withholds is creating and editing alarms. Stopping a ring, snoozing, and turning an alarm off are always free — nobody should have to pay to make their phone stop.
